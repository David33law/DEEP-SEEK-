# NATIONAL LEGAL OBSERVATORY — ACTIVE NOVELTY & META-SEARCH CONTRACT v1

## 1. Purpose

The architecture tournament may not infer design-space exhaustion from repeated scores, repeated family names, elapsed time, budget exhaustion, or a few rounds without an improved incumbent. A supremacy claim requires an active campaign whose purpose is to discover architectures outside the current attractor and to improve the search process itself.

This contract is part of the owner-signed mission. Drift invalidates preflight.

## 2. Controlled architecture identity

The identity used for structural novelty is the canonical ordered tuple of the controlled `class` value for every field in `GENOME_FIELDS`.

Free-form `detail`, family names, prose, implementation language, mechanism wording, hashes of full model output, or cosmetic topology descriptions MUST NOT create novelty.

`controlled_genome_id = SHA-256(canonical JSON of {axis: class})`.

A proposal whose controlled genome already exists is not a new architecture family even if every sentence is different.

## 3. Mandatory active campaign in every escalation round

Before an escalation round can count toward saturation, the runner must execute:

1. at least two independent search-protocol critics;
2. the fixed novelty miner set;
3. every actionable dynamic search directive produced by the critics or carried from the prior round;
4. mechanical controlled-genome deduplication and coverage analysis;
5. construction of a bounded but non-zero set of genuinely novel finalists when any exist;
6. executable semantic and durable-system measurement of constructed novelty candidates through the ordinary frontier path;
7. two independent closure auditors after measurement.

A missing, failed, skipped, schema-invalid, or partially persisted component makes the campaign incomplete and therefore non-dry.

## 4. Fixed novelty miner perspectives

The fixed campaign must include miners that independently attack:

- uncovered controlled genome classes and weakly crossed class pairs;
- the incumbent's load-bearing assumptions by inversion;
- trusted-boundary and authority-seat placement;
- cross-family recombination and incompatible mechanism composition;
- information-theoretic, distributed-systems, legal-evidence, and governance limits;
- the architecture-search protocol itself.

The miners remain blind to prior CP2 conclusions. CP2 continues to enter only through the separately governed post-frontier ceiling comparison.

## 5. Mechanical coverage

Closure requires all non-`other` controlled classes to have appeared in mechanically valid search seeds. For each critical pair of architecture axes, every class on either side must have been explored with at least three distinct counterpart classes.

The critical pairs are:

- canonical authority seat × state derivation model;
- canonical authority seat × consistency/commit model;
- consistency/commit model × replication/distribution model;
- trusted-core topology × provenance/proof model;
- temporal model × normative-effect model;
- publication topology × governance/evolution model.

Coverage is search evidence, not proof of implementation quality. A seed contributes to coverage only if it validates against the controlled schema. A candidate contributes to measured frontier evidence only after construction and executable evaluation.

## 6. Dynamic taxonomy extensions

A controlled class value of `other`, or a proposed load-bearing axis absent from the frozen taxonomy, is an unresolved taxonomy-extension claim. It cannot count as a dry result. It must be either:

- mapped convincingly to an existing controlled class;
- added to the frozen taxonomy through a new owner-signed mission revision; or
- rejected by an explicit, independently reviewed non-distinction argument.

## 7. Dry-campaign rule

A campaign is dry only when all of the following hold simultaneously:

- it is complete;
- it produced zero newly constructed controlled genomes;
- no valid novel seed failed construction or remained unmeasured;
- controlled class and critical-pair breadth coverage are complete;
- both search-protocol critics report no attainable protocol improvement, no new directive, no taxonomy extension, and no unresolved issue;
- both closure auditors independently support a dry result and report no new directive, attainable improvement, coverage gap, or unresolved issue.

Three consecutive complete dry campaigns are required. Any new controlled genome, actionable search directive, protocol improvement, taxonomy-extension claim, unresolved issue, or incomplete campaign resets the count to zero.

## 8. Meta-search obligation

The search process is itself a candidate for improvement. Search-protocol critics must inspect lineages, taxonomy, selection, construction, evaluators, adversaries, and termination logic. A feasible improvement blocks closure until it is implemented or exercised as a dynamic search directive.

The model may propose protocol improvements; it may not silently rewrite the protocol or owner decisions. Code and signed-contract changes remain governed actions.

## 9. Resource termination

Budget, token, call, wall-clock, round, machine, or operator limits are resource backstops only. Reaching one can produce `BEST_DISCOVERED_SO_FAR`; it can never establish `COMMITTED` or a supremacy ceiling.

## 10. Public claim

A public supremacy dossier may state only that no constructible superior architecture was found under the disclosed search, implementation, falsification, durable-systems, lower-bound, and novelty protocols. It must publish the falsifiers that would reopen the result.

It must not claim actual endorsement by Elon Musk, xAI, SpaceX, DeepMind, DARPA, or any other third party. It may explain why a first-principles frontier engineering team would rationally choose the finalist, using measured evidence rather than attributed opinion.
