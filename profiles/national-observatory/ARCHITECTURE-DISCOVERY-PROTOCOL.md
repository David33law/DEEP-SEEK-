# NATIONAL LEGAL OBSERVATORY — WHOLE-SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE DISCOVERY PROTOCOL v2

## 1. Scope and unit of competition

Candidates are complete, executable architecture systems for the National Legal Observatory mission. The unit of competition is never an isolated parser, database, graph, agent, watcher, legal-rule function or storage mechanism.

A candidate must provide one coherent whole-system answer for:

- authoritative-source observation and source-universe accountability;
- immutable primary evidence and admission;
- canonical legal-object identity and version identity;
- effective/legal time and system-knowledge time;
- amendments, repeals, suspensions, revivals, corrections, codifications, renumbering and transitional effects;
- jurisprudence linked to the exact applicable authority version;
- doctrine/theory as a separate epistemic layer;
- proof-carrying provenance, conflicts and explicit unknowns;
- one canonical state projected consistently to human, API, linked-data, ELI, public-sector and AI channels;
- deterministic replay, recovery and clean-machine rebuild;
- governance, evolution, scale, partitioning and migration from the attested target repository.

No legally material source or change may disappear silently.

## 2. Evidence and blindness

The verified CP1 repository reconstruction and exact isolated commit/tree are reusable baseline evidence. They are supplied to discovery so calls are spent on architecture, not repeated archaeology.

Earlier CP2 architecture research remains quarantined during independent search, anti-attractor search, initial expansion and active novelty/meta-search. It may be revealed only after a new executable independent frontier exists, through the dedicated ceiling-analysis path, for missed-family detection and adversarial comparison. CP2 is evidence against the new frontier, never a preselected design.

## 3. No architecture is preselected

Event sourcing, proof DAGs, canonical databases, replicated state machines, declarative legal IRs, verified compilers, content-addressed stores, federated authority meshes, Common Lisp kernels, capability microkernels and polyglot systems are hypotheses. None receives first-answer privilege.

Independent searches may converge. Convergence counts only after the shared assumptions have been attacked by mechanically constrained anti-attractor searches and genuinely different controlled genomes have been constructed and measured.

Every seed and blueprint declares the controlled architecture genome defined in `observatory_roles.py`:

Structural identity is the ordered controlled class vector. Family names, prose, implementation language and hashes of full answers do not create novelty. `other` and `novel_axes` are unresolved taxonomy claims until mapped, added through a new owner-signed revision, or independently rejected as non-distinct.

## 5. Initial search forest

Before the v0 gate, the production path must run:

- six independent first-principles lineages;
- nine anti-attractor lineages that forbid or invert common authority, event-sourcing, CAS, single-writer, replication, proof, compiler, trusted-core or federation assumptions;
- at least six seeds per lineage;
- mechanically checked structural diversity;
- at least twelve structurally distinct selected finalists.

Every lineage records explicit rejected ideas, assumptions, failure modes, decisive advantages and its own evidence-bounded ceiling.

## 6. Complete blueprint and formalization

A selected seed is expanded into a complete blueprint. Controlled classes must survive expansion unchanged.

The blueprint, trusted boundary, all mechanisms, falsifiable predictions, limitations and citations are bound by SHA-256 through explorer → formalizer → builder → evaluator handoff. No lossy summary or first-mechanism extraction is allowed.

Before implementation, each blueprint is formalized into stable `INV-*` obligations, authority and transition semantics, failure conditions, recovery obligations, publication/governance invariants, forbidden shortcuts and explicitly unproved claims. Prebuild destroyers attack the blueprint and formalization but cannot eliminate it without executable evidence.

## 7. Multiple implementations and fidelity

Every finalist receives multiple independent implementation attempts. Visible semantic execution selects the best attempt without exposing hidden labels. Two blueprint-fidelity critics inspect invariant preservation; their prose is advisory, while executable measurement remains authoritative.

A candidate that truncates its blueprint, hard-codes fixtures or substitutes a generic familiar architecture for the declared genome is rejected.

## 8. Two proof arenas

Every architecture must produce:

### A. Semantic kernel

An isolated implementation tested on visible and encrypted hidden replay for identity, bitemporality, normative effects, jurisprudence-version linkage, doctrine separation, provenance, unknown/conflict handling, deterministic replay, publication consistency and governed extension.

### B. Durable system

A separate durable implementation tested in network-disabled, read-only-root containers with an explicit state directory. It faces restart, duplicate delivery, concurrent ingestion, forced process death, corruption, deterministic rebuild, large history and publication-root consistency. Qualification may drive architecture-preserving revisions; independent replication and final crown runs do not permit revision.

No candidate enters the Pareto frontier unless both arenas pass.

## 9. Pareto frontier, not aggregate-score coronation

All hard minima must pass. Candidates are compared on the signed Pareto dimensions; no weighted aggregate can hide a failure. A first survivor is only `PROVISIONAL_FRONTIER_MEMBER`.

Dominated candidates remain evidence. The incumbent must also survive independent hidden replication, evolvability probing and one-time final holdout.

## 10. Escalation challengers

Every escalation round must construct and measure:

- a bottleneck-breaking successor;
- an explicit recombination of measured load-bearing mechanisms;
- a radical architecture differing on multiple controlled axes;
- a minimal trusted-core simplification challenger.

Round-created candidates receive the same complete blueprint, formalization, semantic arena and durable arena obligations as initial finalists.

## 11. Active novelty and meta-search saturation

`NOVELTY-SEARCH-CONTRACT.md` is the binding operational definition.

Every candidate dry wave runs six CP2-direct-blind novelty miners: assumption inversion, morphological-gap search, cross-domain transfer, surgical mutation, trusted-boundary recutting and taxonomy challenge.

The search process itself is then attacked by two independent meta-search critics. Mechanical coverage must include every non-`other` controlled class and critical axis-pair breadth of at least three counterpart classes. Every coverage gap or executable critic directive creates a targeted miner. New controlled genomes are expanded, formalized and constructed; failures remain backlog and are retried.

Two independent closure auditors must reproduce the mechanical facts exactly. Three consecutive complete dry waves are required. Any new genome, admission, backlog, unresolved build, taxonomy claim, protocol improvement, coverage gap, missing component or CP2-direct leak resets the count.

Elapsed time, budget, model agreement, repeated scores or a fixed round count can yield only `BEST_DISCOVERED_SO_FAR`.

## 12. Final falsification and lower bounds

A semantic winner is not yet a national architecture. The final candidate must pass the larger durable crown campaign, three independent lower-bound analyses and independent legal, systems and minimality destroyers.

The lower-bound campaign separates fundamental information, distributed-systems, legal-evidence and governance limits from unfinished engineering. Any feasible unresolved improvement blocks supremacy.

## 13. Public supremacy case

The final dossier must identify the architecture and trusted core, all materially different searched and constructed families, the measurements that killed alternatives, load-bearing mechanisms, injected failures, lower bounds, limitations and falsifiers.

It must explain why a first-principles frontier engineering team would rationally choose the finalist over the strongest measured alternatives. It must not claim actual endorsement by Elon Musk, xAI, SpaceX, DeepMind, DARPA or any other third party without independent evidence.

## 14. Terminal semantics

`COMMITTED` requires all signed semantic, durable, search, novelty, meta-search, coverage, lower-bound, destroyer, audit and public-case conditions to close.

If any attainable improvement, structural family, backlog, unresolved claim, failed proof arena or protocol obligation remains, the strongest valid result is `BEST_DISCOVERED_SO_FAR`. Resource exhaustion is never proof of supremacy.
