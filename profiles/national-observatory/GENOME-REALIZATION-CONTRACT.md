# National Legal Observatory — Controlled Genome Realization Contract v2

## 1. Purpose

A controlled architecture genome is a search-space identity, not proof that the implementation realizes the declared architecture. A candidate that changes labels or constants while retaining one generic mechanism must not receive credit for a distinct family.

Every surviving architecture must therefore prove that all thirteen controlled axes are load-bearing in its actual semantic, durable, distributed, scale, bounded-formal and interoperability artifacts. Citation validity is necessary but is not itself causal evidence; replication and crown additionally require executable source ablation.

## 2. Required axes

The audit covers exactly:

1. canonical authority seat;
2. evidence primitive;
3. identity model;
4. state-derivation model;
5. temporal model;
6. normative-effect model;
7. consistency/commit model;
8. replication/distribution model;
9. trusted-core topology;
10. provenance/proof model;
11. publication topology;
12. governance/evolution model;
13. scaling/partition model.

The class reviewed for each axis must equal the frozen controlled class in the candidate genome. Free-form detail cannot change identity.

## 3. Independent realization auditors

Two independently prompted auditors receive:

- the complete blueprint and formalization;
- an AST-derived definition and symbol census for every candidate source artifact;
- bounded source excerpts;
- hashes of the complete source files;
- persisted semantic, durable, distributed, scale, formal, interoperability and cross-model reports;
- the exact controlled genome.

Auditor A follows an invariant-to-code proof strategy. Auditor B follows a counterexample/removal strategy with an independently sampled response. Different role names alone are insufficient: their per-axis citation maps must differ materially while each map passes the trusted validator independently.

For every axis each auditor must identify:

- concrete AST definitions that implement the choice;
- the `INV-*` obligations governing it;
- persisted executable evidence bearing on it;
- the failure that would occur if the mechanism were removed or replaced;
- a falsifier for the realization claim.

A family name, imported symbol, comment, manifest constant or prose assertion alone is not realization evidence.

## 4. Mechanical citation validation

The trusted validator independently verifies that:

- every axis appears exactly once;
- every reported class equals the candidate genome;
- every cited definition exists in the AST census of the cited artifact;
- every cited invariant exists in the frozen formalization;
- every evidence path exists inside the current runtime and is content-hashed;
- every passing report records the SHA-256 of the exact source bytes it evaluated;
- stale, cross-candidate or failed reports are excluded;
- the required source and evidence groups for that axis are represented;
- each auditor uses a sufficiently diverse definition map rather than collapsing all axes onto generic entrypoints;
- both auditors report no blocker and an overall passing verdict.

A missing, invented, stale or source-mismatched citation rejects the audit even when an auditor says `realized=true`.

## 5. Required artifact groups

At minimum:

- authority, evidence and state derivation require semantic and formal artifacts;
- identity, temporal and normative-effect models require semantic, formal and interoperability artifacts;
- consistency and replication require distributed and formal artifacts;
- scaling requires the scale artifact;
- provenance and publication require semantic and interoperability artifacts;
- governance/evolution requires semantic extension behavior and formal obligations;
- trusted-core topology requires formal and durable executable artifacts.

Both members of each independent formal or interoperability pair are available to the auditors. Each auditor must cite every required group independently.

## 6. Causal source-ablation proof

Citation existence does not prove that a definition is load-bearing. During independent replication and final crown, every auditor-specific axis/group/artifact citation set is therefore tested causally.

For each obligation the trusted ablation engine must:

1. read the exact source bytes identified by the current source census;
2. deterministically rename the complete set of AST definitions cited by that auditor for that axis, group and artifact, leaving call sites unchanged;
3. compile the mutated source;
4. execute the corresponding hidden semantic or specialized durable/distributed/scale/formal/interoperability evaluator under a fresh deterministic seed;
5. persist both the mutated source and evaluator report;
6. verify that the evaluator report records the SHA-256 of the exact mutated source bytes;
7. require the mutation to violate at least one hard property or otherwise fail the signed evaluator.

Every obligation from both independent auditors must be causally falsified. Unioning the two auditor maps into one oversized mutation is insufficient; each auditor's claimed definition set must fail independently.

## 7. Inert negative controls

A failed mutant is meaningful only if failure was caused by the cited mechanism rather than AST rewriting, source hashing or evaluator routing.

Before load-bearing mutations, the engine must run one inert negative control for every affected source artifact. The control appends an unreachable definition, deterministically renames only that unreachable definition, and reruns the same evaluator path. Every control must pass.

A failed control invalidates the causal environment. Infrastructure refusal, timeout, missing output, evaluator invalidation, source-hash drift or malformed reports never count as evidence that an architecture mechanism is load-bearing.

## 8. Qualification, replication and crown

The complete citation/evidence realization audit runs:

- after qualification of every initial and round-created architecture;
- again after independent replication with fresh auditor calls and replication evidence;
- again at the final crown after all crown arenas have completed.

Qualification proves source/evidence binding across the broad field. Replication and crown additionally require the full auditor-specific causal-ablation campaign and inert controls. No architecture revision is allowed during replication or crown realization audit.

## 9. Persisted evidence

Each causal campaign persists:

- candidate and phase identity;
- both auditor identities;
- all required auditor/axis/group obligations;
- every renamed definition set;
- every mutated source path and SHA-256;
- every evaluator receipt path and SHA-256;
- observed pass/fail outcome and bounded diagnostics;
- all inert negative-control outcomes;
- verified axis and obligation counts;
- an explicit proof boundary.

The genome-realization report binds the causal campaign path and SHA-256. The deterministic supremacy dossier indexes the replication and crown causal campaign bytes directly.

## 10. Terminal consequence

The following are explicit supremacy conditions:

- `genome_realization_proven`;
- `genome_realization_replication_passed`;
- `genome_realization_crown_passed`;
- `genome_causal_ablation_replication_passed`;
- `genome_causal_ablation_crown_passed`.

A missing axis, class mismatch, invented definition, missing `INV-*` obligation, stale evidence, generic citation map, auditor dependence, passing load-bearing mutant, failing inert control or infrastructure-only failure blocks `COMMITTED`. Resource exhaustion yields only `BEST_DISCOVERED_SO_FAR`.

The causal proof is bounded to the exact cited source bytes, hidden corpora, fault workloads and definition-set mutations. It does not claim an unbounded theorem about all semantically equivalent rewrites or all future deployments.
