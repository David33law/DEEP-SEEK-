# National Legal Observatory — Active Novelty, Meta-Search & Design-Space Saturation Contract

## 1. Purpose

This contract governs the claim that the architecture search has stopped producing constructible, load-bearing structural alternatives.

A quiet tournament round is not evidence of design-space saturation. A round counts toward saturation only when an explicit CP2-direct-blind novelty campaign has actively searched for previously untried controlled architecture genomes, challenged the search process itself, mechanically examined controlled-class coverage, constructed every selected novel genome, and returned no new buildable genome, no deferred backlog, no unresolved constructibility or taxonomy question, and no attainable search-protocol improvement.

The objective remains the National Legal Observatory: one canonical Greek legal-information infrastructure capable of observing legally material change without silent loss, reconstructing legal-time and knowledge-time state, linking jurisprudence to exact provision versions, separating doctrine from binding law, preserving proof-carrying provenance, rebuilding deterministically and publishing consistent human/API/linked-data/ELI/public-sector/AI projections.

This contract is part of the owner-signed mission. Drift invalidates preflight.

## 2. Controlled structural identity

Architecture novelty is measured by controlled structural classes, not by prose, branding or family names.

The controlled genome axes are the authoritative axes defined by `observatory_roles.py`:

1. canonical authority seat,
2. evidence primitive,
3. identity model,
4. state-derivation model,
5. temporal model,
6. normative-effect model,
7. consistency/commit model,
8. replication/distribution model,
9. trusted-core topology,
10. provenance/proof model,
11. publication topology,
12. governance/evolution model,
13. scaling/partition model.

The canonical genome identifier is derived only from the ordered controlled class vector:

`controlled_genome_id = SHA-256(canonical JSON of {axis: class})`.

Free-form `detail`, family names, implementation language, mechanism wording, hashes of full model output, or cosmetic topology descriptions cannot create novelty. Synonymous descriptions of the same controlled choices are one genome.

## 3. Mandatory active portfolio

Every candidate saturation wave must run all of the following independent search methods without direct access to prior CP2 architecture conclusions:

- **G91 — assumption inversion:** invert the frontier's common load-bearing assumptions and search for mission-preserving alternatives.
- **G92 — morphological gap search:** inspect underrepresented controlled-class combinations and search the gaps rather than the current attractor.
- **G93 — cross-domain transfer:** derive architectures from theorem proving, verified compilation, distributed consensus, scientific evidence systems and other structurally relevant domains without copying product conventions.
- **G94 — surgical genome mutation:** change one or a small number of load-bearing controlled axes around measured frontier members and state the falsifiable advantage.
- **G95 — trusted-boundary recut:** move work across the trusted/untrusted boundary and search for a smaller or differently constituted authoritative kernel.
- **G96 — ontology challenge:** search for mission-preserving structures poorly represented by the current taxonomy and expose every proposed novel axis explicitly.

Each method must produce multiple incompatible seeds, explicit rejected ideas, failure modes, assumptions and an exhaustion note. A missing or malformed method blocks saturation; it is never treated as a dry result.

## 4. Mandatory meta-search

The search process is itself a candidate for improvement. Every novelty wave must run at least two independent search-protocol critics after the six fixed miners and before the wave can be declared dry.

The critics must inspect:

- lineage coverage and blind spots;
- controlled taxonomy adequacy;
- selection and deduplication logic;
- blueprint expansion and formalization;
- implementation diversity;
- semantic and durable evaluators;
- challenger, recombination and simplification logic;
- stopping and supremacy conditions.

A feasible improvement blocks closure until it is either implemented in code or exercised as a dynamic search directive. A new directive must be persisted and executed as an additional CP2-direct-blind miner. The model may propose protocol improvements; it may not silently rewrite the protocol or owner decisions.

## 5. Mechanical coverage obligation

Closure requires all non-`other` controlled classes to have appeared in mechanically valid search seeds.

For each of the following critical axis pairs, every class on either side must have been explored with at least three distinct counterpart classes:

- canonical authority seat × state-derivation model;
- canonical authority seat × consistency/commit model;
- consistency/commit model × replication/distribution model;
- trusted-core topology × provenance/proof model;
- temporal model × normative-effect model;
- publication topology × governance/evolution model.

Coverage is search evidence, not proof of implementation quality. A seed contributes to coverage only if it validates against the controlled schema. A candidate contributes to measured frontier evidence only after construction and executable semantic and durable evaluation.

Any coverage gap must generate a targeted dynamic search directive and therefore makes the current wave non-dry.

## 6. CP2-direct blindness

Novelty miners, meta-search critics and closure auditors must not receive raw prior CP2 content, CP2 adjudications or CP2-derived architecture text as context.

They may observe the current measured frontier because that frontier is contemporary experimental evidence. They may not be shown the historical CP2 source itself. Any later CP2 comparison remains the responsibility of the dedicated ceiling-analysis path.

Every novelty-wave artifact must state `prior_cp2_direct_content_read: false`.

## 7. Discovery, backlog and construction

A controlled genome is not discharged merely because a model named it.

For each novelty wave:

1. all method outputs are normalized to controlled genome identifiers;
2. previously attempted genomes are separated from genuinely unseen genomes;
3. unseen genomes are ranked deterministically by structural distance, mechanical coverage gain and method coverage;
4. selected genomes are expanded into complete blueprints;
5. controlled classes must survive expansion unchanged;
6. the blueprint is formalized into `INV-*` obligations;
7. prebuild destroyers attack it;
8. semantic and durable implementations are constructed through the ordinary production path;
9. unbuilt or deferred genomes remain an explicit backlog;
10. two independent closure auditors inspect the complete measured record.

A deferred or failed-to-construct genome is not silently discarded. It remains a blocker until it is constructed, independently shown mission-infeasible, or carried forward for another attempt.

## 8. Dynamic taxonomy extensions

A controlled class value of `other`, or a proposed load-bearing axis absent from the frozen taxonomy, is an unresolved taxonomy-extension claim. It cannot count as a dry result.

It must be either:

- mapped convincingly to an existing controlled class;
- added to the frozen taxonomy through a new owner-signed mission revision; or
- rejected by an explicit, independently reviewed non-distinction argument.

## 9. Dry-wave definition

A novelty wave is dry only when all of the following are true:

- all six required miners completed;
- both independent meta-search critics completed;
- both independent closure auditors completed and support a dry result;
- no previously unseen controlled genome was produced and constructed;
- the deferred backlog is empty;
- no constructibility question remains unresolved;
- no taxonomy-extension claim remains unresolved;
- mechanical class and critical-pair breadth coverage are complete;
- no critic or auditor produced an attainable protocol improvement or new search directive;
- no direct prior-CP2 content was read;
- the wave artifact and its controlled-genome evidence were persisted.

Three consecutive complete dry novelty waves are required before `genome_saturated` may become true.

Any new controlled genome, dynamic directive, attainable protocol improvement, coverage gap, taxonomy-extension claim, deferred backlog, unresolved build failure, missing component or CP2-direct leak resets the dry-wave count to zero.

## 10. Resource termination

Budget, token, call, wall-clock, round, machine or operator limits are resource backstops only. Reaching one can produce `BEST_DISCOVERED_SO_FAR`; it can never establish `COMMITTED` or a supremacy ceiling.

## 11. Terminal consequence

`COMMITTED` is forbidden unless active novelty and meta-search saturation are proven in addition to the other supremacy conditions.

If active novelty closure is absent, the strongest permitted terminal label is `BEST_DISCOVERED_SO_FAR`.

## 12. Public claim discipline

The final supremacy case may state only that the selected architecture is the highest evidence-supported constructible architecture found under the disclosed search, construction, falsification, durable-systems, lower-bound, active-novelty and meta-search protocols.

It may not claim actual endorsement by Elon Musk, xAI, SpaceX, DeepMind, DARPA or any other third party. It must instead explain, with falsifiable engineering evidence, why a first-principles frontier team would rationally choose the architecture over the measured alternatives and what future evidence would overturn that conclusion.
